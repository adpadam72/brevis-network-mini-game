Brevis Sling — a Brevis-themed Angry Birds mini-game

Drag the BREVIS orb, pull and release to launch. Knock over the data-blocks!

This mini-game is styled for Brevis (a ZK omnichain data-attestation platform) — project blocks represent data attestation nodes and the orb is a Brevis proof token. See source for embedding on your page.

Tip: Press space to spawn a new orb after launching. Built to drop into an index.html on your site.
